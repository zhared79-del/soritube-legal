A Quiet Morning Walk

Every morning, Mina walks to the small park near her home. The air is cool, and the streets are still quiet. She puts on her earphones, listens to an article she saved the night before, and watches the sky slowly turn bright.

Walking helps her think clearly. Some days she plans her work. Other days she simply enjoys the moment and lets her mind rest.

On the way back, she buys warm bread from a bakery on the corner. The baker always smiles and asks about her day.

By the time she gets home, she feels calm and ready. A short walk is a simple habit, but it gives her energy for the whole day.
